How the videos are made

Each video is researched from cited sources, scripted, and assembled with the help of AI tools for visuals and narration. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published, and videos containing realistic AI-generated content are labeled using YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.

AndItFigures Studio

AndItFigures Studio is our private, internal production tool. It is used only by the channel owner to produce videos and upload them to channels the owner operates. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube account. It uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy for details.
