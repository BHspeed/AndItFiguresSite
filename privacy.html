Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy describes how AndItFigures Studio ("the Studio"), the internal video production tool operated by the owner of the AndItFigures and RobloxBlicky YouTube channels, handles data.

YouTube API Services

The Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using the Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the Studio

The Studio is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, to publish videos to YouTube channels the owner operates. It is not available to the public and does not request access to any other person's Google or YouTube account.

Information accessed

Through the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's authorization, the Studio:

  • uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and publishing settings to the owner's channels;
  • reads basic information about the owner's own channels (channel name and ID) and the status of videos it uploaded (for example, processing and privacy status), to confirm uploads succeeded.

The Studio does not access viewer data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics about individual users, or any data belonging to other YouTube users.

How information is used, stored, and shared

  • Information is used only to publish and verify the owner's own videos.
  • OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.
  • A local log of uploaded video IDs, titles, and publish times is kept on the owner's computer to schedule posts and avoid duplicate topics.
  • No YouTube API data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

The Studio does not place cookies on, or collect information from, viewers' devices. This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored data is kept only as long as needed to operate the channels. The owner can delete it at any time by removing the Studio's local data folder. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, after which the Studio can no longer access the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
